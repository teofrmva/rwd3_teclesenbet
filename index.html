Reflection

I used clamp(30px, 5vw, 58px) for my H1 title. The 30px keeps it readable on phones. The 5vw helps it grow with the screen. The 58px stops it from getting too big. I also changed the other text sizes. I tested my website on phone, tablet, and desktop sizes to make sure everything looked good.
